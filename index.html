I am not the mind, nor the intellect, nor the ego, nor the memory. I am not the ears, nor the tongue, nor the nose, nor the eyes. I am not the sky, nor the earth, nor fire, nor air. I am the form of Consciousness and Bliss. I am Shiva, I am Shiva. I am not the breath, nor the five elements, nor the seven dhatus, nor the five sheaths. I am not speech, nor hands, nor feet, nor the organs of action. I am ever pure blissful consciousness. I am Shiva, I am Shiva. I have no hatred or dislike, no attachment or greed, no pride or arrogance, no jealousy at all. I have no duty, no wealth, no desire nor liberation. I am the form of Consciousness and Bliss. I am Shiva, I am Shiva. I am neither virtue nor vice, neither pleasure nor pain, neither mantra nor pilgrimage, neither Vedas nor yajnas. I am not food, nor the eater, nor the act of eating. I am the form of Consciousness and Bliss. I am Shiva, I am Shiva. I have no fear of death, no doubt about my existence, no bondage, no caste, no creed, no father, no mother, no birth, no relation. I am the form of Consciousness and Bliss. I am Shiva, I am Shiva. I am without any variation, without form or qualities. I am all-pervading. I am beyond senses. I am always the same. I am pure knowledge and bliss. I am Shiva, I am Shiva.
